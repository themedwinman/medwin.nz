— A bit more about me

Off the résumé.

I’m Oli, a cybersecurity and networks student at AUT in Auckland. I run a Raspberry Pi homelab where I manage DNS filtering with Pi-hole, VPN access with Tailscale, and self-hosted services including a Jellyfin media server, an arr stack, and Vaultwarden — all behind proper access control on a private network.

I’ve built a personal finance dashboard that aggregates my accounts through Akahu, New Zealand’s open banking API, into a single view. I’m also running a VPS to start hosting my own MCP servers, extending the same infrastructure work into AI tooling.

I’ve also built and deployed a full-stack house points tracking app with Google OAuth and role-based access control, now in use at Kingsway.

Outside of that I teach drums, play in a band called Last Minute, and work behind the bar at Shed 16.

Currently looking for a cybersecurity internship where I can apply what I’ve built at home to real systems.
